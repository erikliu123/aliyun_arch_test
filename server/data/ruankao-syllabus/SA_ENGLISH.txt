系统架构设计师考纲 — 专业英语

一、考试形式
- 5道英语阅读理解选择题（每题1分，共5分）
- 通常是一篇技术文章，设置5个填空或理解题
- 涉及IT领域专业词汇和概念

二、常考技术词汇领域
- 软件工程：requirement, specification, prototype, iteration, agile, refactoring
- 架构设计：architecture, component, module, scalability, availability, fault-tolerance
- 数据库：transaction, concurrency, normalization, index, schema, integrity
- 网络：protocol, bandwidth, latency, firewall, encryption, authentication
- 云计算：virtualization, containerization, microservice, serverless, elasticity
- 安全：vulnerability, exploit, patch, cipher, certificate, authorization

三、常见技术概念英文表述
- Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA)
- Model-View-Controller (MVC)
- Representational State Transfer (REST)
- Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)
- Domain-Driven Design (DDD)
- Test-Driven Development (TDD)

四、阅读技巧
- 理解上下文推断词义
- 技术文档结构：Abstract→Introduction→Body→Conclusion
- 因果关系词：therefore, consequently, due to, as a result
- 对比关系词：however, whereas, in contrast, on the other hand
